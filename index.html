    The Perfect Roast Chicken: A Timeless Sunday Ritual

    Golden skin, juicy meat, and a pan sauce that captures every drop of flavour.
    by Clara Fontana · updated 12 Mar 2026 · 15 min read

    Roast chicken is the ultimate test of a home cook’s intuition. It’s not about a single secret; it’s about temperature, timing, and a little patience. This guide will walk you through every step—from choosing the bird to carving it at the table.

    Why Dry Brining Changes Everything

    Salt the chicken 24 hours ahead. Place it on a rack in the fridge, uncovered. The skin dries, the salt penetrates, and the result is a crackling‑crisp exterior and deeply seasoned meat.

    The 30‑Minute Rest

    Let the chicken rest for at least 30 minutes before carving. This allows the juices to redistribute, ensuring every slice is moist and flavourful.

    Bird weightRoast time (180°C)Internal temp
    1.2 – 1.5 kg60 – 70 min74°C
    1.8 – 2.0 kg75 – 85 min74°C
    2.2 – 2.5 kg90 – 100 min74°C
    👨‍🍳 Chef’s tip
    Add a halved lemon and a few sprigs of thyme inside the cavity. The steam will perfume the meat from within.

    Pan Sauce: Liquid Gold

    While the chicken rests, place the roasting pan over medium heat. Add a splash of white wine or stock, scrape up the browned bits, and reduce until syrupy. Finish with a knob of butter.

    Can I use a different bird?

    Absolutely. This method works beautifully for guinea fowl, Cornish hens, or even a small turkey.

    What if I don’t have a roasting rack?

    Use thick slices of onion or carrot as a makeshift rack — they also add flavour to the pan sauce.

    Ready to roast? This is more than a recipe — it’s a ritual. Gather your people, pour a glass of wine, and let the oven do its work.
