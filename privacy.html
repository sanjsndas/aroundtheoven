    Frequently asked

    Quick answers to the most common questions we receive.

    Do you accept guest recipes?

    Absolutely! We love hearing from the community. Send us your recipe and story through the form — we’ll be in touch.

    Can I republish your content?

    We appreciate your interest! Please reach out for permission — we usually say yes with proper attribution and a link back.

    Do you offer baking classes?

    Not yet, but we’re planning a series of small online workshops for 2027. Sign up for our newsletter to be the first to know.

    How can I support your work?

    Sharing our posts, leaving a comment, or buying us a coffee via the link in our bio helps us keep the oven burning!
